# Prior art before design

- During reconnaissance, ask the user in one line whether to study prior art (and, for UI or flows, build a clickable prototype) and why it would help. Do it only on a yes; skip the offer for small edits that follow an existing convention.
- Study 3-5 best-in-class products and any common standard: the pattern each uses, and what its users complain about (docs, issue trackers, reviews). Cite sources; separate evidence from opinion.
- Design your own answer: keep what is proven, avoid the known pains, improve where you can. Do not copy a product wholesale.
- For UI, design phone and desktop together and every state (empty, error, offline, long or many items); the prototype lets the user try it before the contract.
- Record the findings, sources and resulting decisions in the feature contract's Prior art section.
